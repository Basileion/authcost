What is an active tenant? A tenant is active if two or more users are associated with the tenant and at least one user logs in within a month. Descope only charges for active tenants. What is an SSO connection? An SSO connection is any connection between a tenant and an identity provider using SAML / OIDC SSO to access your B2B application.
