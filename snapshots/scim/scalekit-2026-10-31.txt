Free $0/month: 25,000 MAUs (auto-upgrades to Growth after), 25 organizations, 1 SSO & 1 SCIM connection.
Growth $99/month: 100,000 MAUs + $0.05 per additional user; 100 organizations + $1 per additional org.
SSO beyond 1 connection: 2-15 $60/conn/mo, 16-30 $45, 31-50 $35, 51-100 $30. SCIM: same ladder.
FAQ: "Your first connection of each type is free ... Example: enable 3 SSO connections and you pay for 2 (the first is free), so $120/month. SSO and SCIM are priced separately, each with its own free connection."
"The Free plan is a hard cap with no overages. When you hit 25,000 MAUs, 25 orgs, or need more than 1 SSO connection, you upgrade to Growth."
100+ connections: Let's talk (Custom).
